## Primary-source clarification for FR02

The retained Bloch–Kato proof identifies [Fo] as Fontaine's [1982 Annals paper](https://annals.math.princeton.edu/1982/115-3/p03), pp.529–577. Its requested locks include the period-ring input cited in Proposition 1.17 and the finite-height formal-group comparison cited in Example 3.10.1. Example 3.11 uses abelian-variety de Rhamness, a semistable-reduction step, and, for its local-condition equality, the vanishing of the relevant crystalline Frobenius-fixed space via special-fiber weights. These are narrower interfaces than all of p-adic Hodge theory, but stronger than Hodge–Tate comparison alone. The same paper's §1 also cites Fontaine–Messing for period foundations; its actually retained slice still needs selection. [Bloch–Kato, pp.340–341, 359–361 and bibliography p.399](https://math.stanford.edu/~conrad/BSDseminar/refs/BKTamagawa.pdf).

This review located the requests and bibliographic identity; it did not freshly inspect the original Fontaine theorem statements. Keep them caller-audited until that check succeeds.

## Checks that did not produce a defect

- **Riemann–Roch and Belyi:** [F07](../sources/foundations/algebraic-curves.md) and R07 retain smooth projective geometrically integral curves, rather than a false unrestricted curve formula. They explicitly include section separation and linear systems, which the Noncritical Belyi proof needs. The source-selection task appropriately chooses Hartshorne or Liu rather than counting both whole texts.
- **Local CFT:** [F03](../sources/foundations/class-field-theory.md) distinguishes local reciprocity/existence from global reconstruction, preserves unit/inertia and Frobenius normalization, and explicitly owns Milne III.3.2's unfinished displayed proof. The missing-proof notice was corroborated in the current primary PDF. The [NSW card](../sources/foundations/continuous-galois-cohomology.md) and P16 now own deeper global arithmetic leaves instead of inferring Neukirch–Uchida from local reciprocity.
- **Tate 1967:** [F11](../sources/foundations/tate-p-divisible.md) deliberately leaves the precise low-degree/twist computations to an original-statement lock and warns about the exceptional untwisted case. It makes no blanket continuous-cohomology vanishing claim. Original proofs remain unreviewed here.
- **Faltings/Faltings–Chai:** [F10](../sources/foundations/faltings-p-adic-hodge.md) retains relative/integral uniformity rather than substituting rational comparison. [F13](../sources/foundations/faltings-chai.md) explicitly separates prime-to-residue-characteristic statements from possible `ℓ = p` uses and distinguishes semistable hypotheses from existence of semistable reduction. Their pending original-theorem locks are genuine remaining research tasks, not completed source audits.
- **Finite-field weights:** [F14](../sources/foundations/abelian-varieties-weights.md)'s accessible Milne II.1.1(b), p.75, is the abelian-variety Weil/RH theorem requested by the route. The card properly retains Jacobian/polarization/weight-filtration work and does not import the full Weil conjectures for arbitrary varieties.
- **Analytic frameworks:** [F17](../sources/foundations/rigid-analytic-foundations.md) and [F18](../sources/foundations/berkovich-analytic-topology.md) distinguish rigid functions/gluing from the analytic topology used in topological coverings. They do not substitute the topology of rational points or the étale site for Berkovich topology. Their precise cover/analytification proof leaves remain owned selection tasks.
- **Reusable deliverables:** R01–R26 have conventional standalone interfaces and non-IUT consumers/applications. R27–R28 are explicitly described as more specialized. The catalogue requires actual proofs and interpretation bridges, so conditional prototypes do not count as releases.

## Parallelism check

The early assignments in the roadmap are credible as **definition/API work**, with conditional consumers explicitly separated from accepted mathematical outputs. The staged P13, P17 and P27/P28 handoffs prevent several artificial serial dependencies.

With F19 added, preserve a staged P05 boundary: finite-cover core first, curve/coherent definitions in P06, then the needed geometric-cohomology outputs for P09/P10. Similarly, P16's local invariants can be released before its global Neukirch–Uchida theorem where a consumer needs only the local output.

The plan is suitable for parallel team assignment once these ownership/edge clarifications are incorporated. It remains a high-level plan with explicit theorem-source closure work; it is not a certificate that the entire recursive prerequisite list or any Lean proof has already been completed.
