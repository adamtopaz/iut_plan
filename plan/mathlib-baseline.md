# Mathlib baseline and reuse boundary

**Planning baseline:** mathlib4 [`e91b128082cc221d6432a6a5aadcd3243af8e76c`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/commit/e91b128082cc221d6432a6a5aadcd3243af8e76c), committed **2026-10-15T13:41:48Z**, required toolchain **Lean4.35.0**. The survey searched8,631 Mathlib Lean files and inspected the declarations below. This is source inspection, not a compiled Lean validation; P01 must recheck the baseline before implementation begins.

“Available” means the cited interface exists under its own hypotheses. “Partial” means useful infrastructure exists but not the complete consumer theorem. “Not located” records a scoped search; it does not prove that no equivalent theorem is derivable.

A major positive result is the abstract Galois-category equivalence. Reuse it when constructing geometric pi1. Conversely, etale sites/ell-adic cohomology definitions do not already prove the needed geometric base-change/duality or arithmetic comparison theorems.

## Large interfaces to implement or re-audit

- **P02:** arbitrary finite-local-extension integration and local-unit logarithm kernel/image/functoriality.
- **P03–P04:** continuous Kummer, its inverse systems, local reciprocity and selected local duality.
- **P05–P06:** connected-scheme finite-etale Galois categories, arithmetic exact sequence, selected geometric cohomology and curve duality/linear systems.
- **P07–P10:** geometric elliptic/abelian/Jacobian interfaces, Tate uniformization, analytic topology, finite-field weights and the selected p-adic comparisons.
- **P11–P20:** tree/profinite/anabelioid refinements, relative/tempered anabelian results, cuspidalization/reconstruction, Frobenioids and EtTh rigidity.
- **P21–P31:** IUT definitions, constructions, interpretation/naturality laws and final theorem assembly.

Whole-tree searches did not locate implementations under the names anabelian, Frobenioid, tempered groups/covers, Neukirch-Uchida, local reciprocity/class field theory, Tate curve/uniformization, Belyi, Berkovich, rigid analytic, or Kummer map/sequence. False friends such as complex Jacobi theta, topological fundamental groups, tempered distributions, Tate cohomology and Fontaine theta were separated. Exact import/hypothesis matching in P01 can revise these grades.

## Reproducible implementation check

At the start of a formalization package, record the selected Lean/mathlib commit, exact import paths/declarations and a compiling example of the required interface. Before adding a new definition or theorem, search current mathlib and active upstream/downstream projects. Where an existing result differs in coefficient category, topology, normalization or hypotheses, record and prove the bridge rather than counting the interface as fully supplied.

Keep existing axioms of ordinary Lean foundations separate from additional mathematical assumptions. Final P31 must compile with the retained mathematics proved, not with a collection of opaque anabelian or IUT axioms.
